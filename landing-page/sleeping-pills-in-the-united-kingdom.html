How to identify counterfeit medications

Fake sleeping pills can be dangerous because they may contain the wrong ingredient, incorrect dosage, or harmful substances. Before buying any medicine online, always check the seller, prescription process, and packaging carefully.

  • Check that the online pharmacy provides clear business and contact details.
  • Avoid websites that sell prescription-only medicines without any prescription or review.
  • Look for sealed packaging, batch number, and expiry date when medicine arrives.
  • Be careful with prices that look unrealistically cheap.
  • Do not use medicine if the packaging looks damaged, misspelled, or suspicious.
  • Speak with a pharmacist or doctor if you are unsure about a sleeping medicine.

FAQ about sleeping pills

Can I buy sleeping pills online in the UK?

Yes, you can buy some sleeping pills online in the UK through a responsible pharmacy process. However, many sleeping medicines are prescription-only, so you may need a valid prescription or clinical review before the order is approved.

Do I need a prescription for sleeping pills?

Many stronger sleeping tablets require a prescription because they may cause side effects, dependency or interactions with other medicines. Always follow medical advice and avoid any website that sells prescription-only sleeping pills without proper checks.

Are sleeping pills safe for daily use?

Sleeping pills are usually intended for short-term use unless a doctor advises otherwise. Daily use without medical guidance can increase the risk of tolerance, dependency and side effects, so customers should always follow professional advice.

What should I check before ordering sleeping pills online?

Before ordering, check the pharmacy details, product information, prescription process, dosage guidance, expiry date and packaging quality. Do not use medicines from suspicious websites or sellers that avoid proper health checks.
