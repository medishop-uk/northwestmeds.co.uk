How to check fake medicines

Fake medicines can be dangerous because they may contain the wrong ingredient, incorrect strength, or harmful substances. Before buying medicine online, always check the pharmacy, packaging, and prescription process carefully.

  • Check if the online pharmacy provides clear contact and business information.
  • Avoid websites that sell prescription-only medicine without any prescription or review.
  • Look for sealed packaging, batch numbers, and expiry dates when the medicine arrives.
  • Be careful with prices that look too cheap or unrealistic.
  • Do not use medicine if the packaging looks damaged, misspelled, or suspicious.
  • Speak to a pharmacist or healthcare professional if you are unsure about a medicine.

Can I buy anxiety medicines online in the UK?

Yes, you can order anxiety-related medicines online in the UK through a responsible pharmacy process. However, many anxiety medicines are prescription-only, so you may need a valid prescription or clinical review before the order is approved.

Do I need a prescription for anxiety medicine?

Many anxiety medicines require a prescription because they can have side effects, interactions or dependency risks. Always follow medical advice and avoid any website that offers prescription-only medicines without proper checks.

Is it safe to order medicines through WhatsApp?

WhatsApp can be useful for order support and communication, but medicine orders should still follow safe pharmacy steps. You should provide accurate details, follow prescription requirements and confirm all product information before completing an order.

How do I know if an online pharmacy is genuine?

A genuine online pharmacy should provide clear contact information, product details, prescription guidance and responsible medicine support. Avoid websites with no pharmacy details, no prescription checks or unrealistic medicine claims.
