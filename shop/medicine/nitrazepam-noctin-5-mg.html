Noctin 5 mg side effects[e]

Nitrazepam may cause side effects, especially when taken in higher doses or for a long period. Most side effects are related to its sedative action on the brain and nervous system. Patients should use it carefully and only under medical supervision. Here are some side effects given below:

  • Daytime drowsiness and excessive sleepiness
  • Dizziness or feeling lightheaded
  • Poor coordination and slower reaction time
  • Memory and concentration problems
  • Muscle weakness or fatigue
  • Dry mouth or blurred vision
  • Risk of dependence with long-term use

FAQ about nitrazepam[g]

What is Noctin 5 mg used for?

It is mainly used for the short-term treatment of insomnia and severe sleep disturbances. It is usually prescribed when sleep problems seriously affect daily life.

How does Noctin 5 mg help with sleep?

It works by calming the brain and nervous system, helping users fall asleep faster and stay asleep longer. It enhances GABA activity, which naturally slows brain activity.

Can Noctin 5 mg cause dependency?

Yes, long-term or excessive use may lead to physical or psychological dependence. Stopping it suddenly after long use may also cause withdrawal symptoms.

Should Noctin 5 mg be taken every night?

It should only be used as prescribed by a doctor and usually for a short duration. Regular long-term use is not recommended due to tolerance risk.

What are the common side effects of Noctin 5 mg?

Common side effects include drowsiness, dizziness, fatigue, and reduced concentration. Some people may also feel groggy the next morning.[h]
