Pregabalin (Pregacare NT)

Pregacare-NT is indicated for neuropathic pain from diabetic neuropathy and postherpetic neuralgia. Its active ingredients—mecobalamin, pregabalin, and nortriptyline—work together to repair nerves and reduce pain. Side effects such as dizziness, drowsiness, nausea, blurred vision, and swelling are usually temporary.
