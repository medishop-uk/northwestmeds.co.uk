FAQ about Pregabalin (Pregacare NT)

What is pregabalin (Pregacare NT) used for?

It’s prescribed for nerve pain caused by conditions like diabetes, spinal issues, or injury. The “NT” combo usually includes nortriptyline, which boosts pain control and helps with nerve-related discomfort.

How should Pregacare NT be taken?

Take it exactly as prescribed, usually once daily at night. Swallow whole with water—don’t crush or self-adjust the dose. This isn’t a DIY medication.

What are the common side effects?

You might feel drowsy, dizzy, or dry-mouthed or experience and mild weight gain. Most side effects ease over time. If symptoms hit hard or don’t fade, check in with your doctor—no ghosting your health.
