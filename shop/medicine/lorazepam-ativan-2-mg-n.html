Side effect of Ativan 2 mg

Lorazepam can help with anxiety and short-term calming needs, but it may cause side effects in some people. Always use Lorazepam under medical supervision and speak to a doctor if side effects continue or feel serious. Here are some side effects:

  • Drowsiness or feeling very tired during the day.
  • Dizziness, lightheadedness, or poor balance.
  • Muscle weakness or reduced coordination.
  • Confusion, memory problems, or unusual mood changes.
  • Dependence or withdrawal risk with long-term or improper use.

FAQ about Lorazepam (Ativan 2mg)

What is lorazepam (Ativan 2 mg) used for?

It’s commonly prescribed for anxiety disorders, short-term severe stress, panic attacks, and sometimes insomnia linked to anxiety. It helps calm the brain fast.

How quickly does Ativan 2 mg work?

Pretty quick. Most people feel effects within 30–60 minutes. That’s why it’s used for short-term relief—not as a long-term daily fix.

Is Lorazepam 2 mg safe to use daily?

Only if a doctor says so. Long-term use or misuse can cause dependence. Common side effects include drowsiness, dizziness, and poor concentration. No mixing with alcohol—ever.

Can Lorazepam Ativan 2mg cause drowsiness?

Yes, drowsiness, dizziness, and reduced alertness are common effects. Avoid driving, alcohol, or operating machinery after taking it.

Is Lorazepam 2mg addictive?

Yes, Lorazepam can be habit-forming if used regularly or without proper supervision. Do not stop suddenly without medical advice.
