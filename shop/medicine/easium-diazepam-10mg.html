Side effects of diazepam (Easium 10 mg)

Easium 10 mg may be helpful, but there are risks associated with it; abuse or prolonged use may make the issue worse. Always take it under proper medical guidance. A few side effects are the following:

  • Drowsiness and excessive sleepiness
  • Dizziness or lightheadedness
  • Confusion or poor concentration
  • Muscle weakness
  • Slurred speech or slowed coordination
  • Dependence and withdrawal symptoms with long use
  • Breathing problems (especially with high doses or alcohol)

What is Easium 10 mg used for?
