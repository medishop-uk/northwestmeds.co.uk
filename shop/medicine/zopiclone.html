Key features of zopiclone

Zopiclone is designed to provide short-term support for individuals struggling with insomnia. Below are some important features and safety considerations:

  • Helps treat insomnia: Supports faster sleep onset and longer sleep duration.
  • Dosage guidance: Adults are usually prescribed 3.75 mg to 7.5 mg before bedtime, depending on medical advice.
  • Pregnancy warning: Zopiclone is generally not recommended during pregnancy unless specifically advised by a healthcare professional.
  • Breastfeeding caution: Small amounts may pass into breast milk, so doctors usually advise avoiding it while breastfeeding.
  • Short-term treatment: Typically prescribed for a few days to a few weeks to prevent dependence.

Side effect of sleep medicine

Zopiclone may help with short-term sleep problems, but it can also cause side effects in some people. These effects can vary depending on the dose, health condition, and how the medicine is taken. Here are some side effects:

  • Drowsiness or sleepiness the next day
  • Bitter or metallic taste in the mouth
  • Dry mouth or throat discomfort
  • Dizziness, headache, or light-headed feeling
  • Confusion, memory problems, or unusual mood changes

FAQ about Zopiclone (7.5 mg/10 mg/20 mg)

What is the usual dose of Zopiclone?

The common adult dose is 7.5 mg taken before bedtime, although some patients may be prescribed 3.75 mg. Higher strengths should only be taken if prescribed by a doctor.

Can Zopiclone cause side effects?

Yes, possible side effects include dizziness, dry mouth, headache, and a bitter taste. Some people may also feel sleepy, confused, or less alert the next day.

How long can you take Zopiclone?

Doctors usually prescribe it for short-term use, typically a few days to a few weeks. Long-term use may increase the risk of dependence, tolerance, or withdrawal symptoms.

Is Zopiclone 10 mg or 20 mg stronger than 7.5 mg?

Yes, 10 mg and 20 mg are stronger than the usual 7.5 mg dose. These higher doses should only be used under proper medical supervision.

Can I take Zopiclone every night?

Zopiclone is usually not recommended for daily long-term use. It is mainly used for short-term insomnia treatment when advised by a doctor.

Does Zopiclone make you feel sleepy the next day?

Yes, some people may feel drowsy, tired, dizzy, or less focused the next day. Avoid driving or using machinery if you still feel sleepy after taking it.
