GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

General information cannot replace advice from your prescriber or pharmacist.

How is nerve pain different from ordinary pain?

Nerve pain results from irritation or damage affecting nerves and may feel burning, shooting, tingling or numb rather than aching.

How quickly can treatment work?

Response times vary by medicine and individual. Some treatments require gradual dose adjustment under clinical guidance.

Can nerve-pain medicine be stopped suddenly?

Some treatments need to be reduced gradually. Follow the advice of the prescribing clinician.
