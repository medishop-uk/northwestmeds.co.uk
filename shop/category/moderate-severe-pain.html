GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

General information cannot replace advice from your prescriber or pharmacist.

Which medicine is appropriate for pain?

The correct treatment depends on the type and cause of pain. A prescriber should assess whether medicine is necessary and which option is suitable.

Can pain medicines cause dependence?

Some prescription pain medicines carry dependence, tolerance and withdrawal risks. Use them only at the prescribed dose and duration.

When does pain need urgent assessment?

Seek urgent medical help for sudden severe pain, chest pain, serious injury, weakness, breathing difficulty or other alarming symptoms.
