GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

General information cannot replace advice from your prescriber or pharmacist.

Can the same medicine be used for anxiety and seizures?

Certain medicines may have more than one licensed or clinically accepted use, but suitability depends on the individual diagnosis and prescriber guidance.

Do seizure medicines have side effects?

All medicines can cause side effects. Read the patient leaflet and discuss unexpected or serious symptoms with a healthcare professional.

Can treatment be stopped immediately?

Stopping some seizure or anxiety medicines suddenly can be unsafe. Always follow a clinician-led reduction plan.
