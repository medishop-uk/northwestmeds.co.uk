GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

General information cannot replace advice from your prescriber or pharmacist.

What are wakefulness medicines used for?

They may be prescribed for eligible patients with specific conditions affecting alertness or daytime wakefulness. A qualified prescriber must decide whether treatment is appropriate.

Can I request these medicines without a prescription?

No. Prescription-only medicines require a valid prescription and the necessary eligibility checks before they can be supplied.

How should these medicines be taken?

Use them only as directed by your prescriber and read the supplied patient information leaflet.
