GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

General information cannot replace advice from your prescriber or pharmacist.

What medicines may be used for anxiety or panic symptoms?

A prescriber may consider different treatments depending on the symptoms, diagnosis, other medicines and medical history.

Are anxiety medicines suitable for long-term use?

Some medicines are intended only for short-term use because tolerance and dependence can occur. Follow your prescriber’s instructions.

Can anxiety medicine be stopped suddenly?

Some medicines should not be stopped abruptly. Ask the prescribing clinician for an appropriate reduction plan.
