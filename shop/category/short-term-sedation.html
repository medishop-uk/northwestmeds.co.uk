GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

General information cannot replace advice from your prescriber or pharmacist.

What are sedative medicines used for?

They may be used for selected short-term conditions or before certain procedures when a qualified clinician considers them appropriate.

Can I drive after taking a sedative?

Sedatives can impair coordination, judgement and alertness. Do not drive or operate machinery when affected, and follow the medicine leaflet.

Can sedatives be combined with alcohol?

No. Alcohol can significantly increase sedation and breathing risks and should be avoided.
