Most orders to Chester CH postcodes are delivered within 1–2 working days via tracked Royal Mail. We also offer a next-day tracked courier option for urgent prescriptions, available when you order before our daily cut-off time.

Do I need to see a GP before ordering from NorthwestMedsUK?

No. For many treatments, you can complete our free online consultation and receive a prescription decision from one of our UK-registered clinicians on the same day — no GP referral or prior appointment needed.

What conditions and treatments can I order online through NorthwestMedsUK?
