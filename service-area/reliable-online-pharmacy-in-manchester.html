FAQ about online pharmacy

Is a prescription always necessary when ordering medication from an online pharmacy?

For prescription-only medications, a valid prescription from a licensed healthcare professional is always required. Some online pharmacies also offer an online doctor service where a consultation process is reviewed by their clinical team to issue a prescription if appropriate.

Do you deliver to my area in Manchester?

We deliver across all ten boroughs of Greater Manchester, including the city centre, Salford, Trafford, Didsbury, Chorlton, Stockport, Bolton, Oldham, Rochdale, and more. Orders placed before 3pm are typically dispatched the same day, with standard delivery arriving within 24–48 hours. All orders are sent in discreet, unmarked packaging.

How do I get a prescription through your online pharmacy?

Simply complete a short online consultation questionnaire about your symptoms and medical history. A registered clinician will review your answers and, if appropriate, issue a prescription directly to our pharmacy.

Can I use my existing NHS prescription with NorthwestMeds UK?

Yes, we accept both NHS and private prescriptions. If you already have a valid NHS prescription from your GP, you can submit it to us for dispensing.

Is my personal and medical information kept private?

Absolutely. We take your privacy seriously. All data is stored securely and handled in accordance with UK GDPR regulations.
