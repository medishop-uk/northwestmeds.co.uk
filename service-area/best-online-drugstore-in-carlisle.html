Online pharmacy delivery can be safe when the medicine is genuine, properly packed, and supplied through a responsible process. Customers should avoid websites that sell prescription medicines without checks.

Can I order anxiety or sleeping tablets online?

Some anxiety and sleeping medicines are prescription-only in the UK. You should only order them after proper medical guidance, prescription checks, or clinical approval.
