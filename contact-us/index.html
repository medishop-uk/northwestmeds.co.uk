FAQs

How can I contact NorthwestMeds?

You can contact us through the available email, phone, WhatsApp, or website inquiry form listed on this page.

Can I ask about an existing order?

Yes. Please provide your name and order reference so our team can locate the relevant information more easily.

Can I contact NorthwestMeds through WhatsApp?

Where WhatsApp support is available, you can use the listed WhatsApp contact details to send general inquiries and order-related questions.

How quickly will I receive a response?

Response times may vary depending on the nature of your inquiry and when it is submitted. We aim to review enquiries as soon as reasonably possible during normal customer-support hours.

Can I get medical advice through the contact form?

The contact form can be used for general inquiries, but it should not be relied upon for urgent medical advice or emergencies. Depending on your question, you may need to speak directly with an appropriately qualified healthcare professional.
