Do I need a prescription to send a request with NorthwestMeds?

Absolutely. UK law requires a valid NHS or private prescription for all prescription-only medicines. Simply upload your prescription during checkout, and our pharmacy team will verify it.

How fast is delivery within the UK?

We offer Royal Mail Tracked and DPD delivery options. Orders are usually dispatched the same day once your prescription is approved, with delivery typically taking 1–2 working days.

Is my order packaged discreetly?

Yes. All orders from NorthwestMeds are shipped in plain, unmarked packaging to protect your privacy. No medication names or pharmacy details appear on the outside.

Are the medicines genuine and UK-approved?

100%. We only source medication from authorised UK wholesalers that meet MHRA standards. Every product is genuine, sealed, and handled by qualified pharmacy professionals.

Can I speak to a pharmacist if I have questions about my medication?

Yes. NorthwestMeds provides access to UK-registered pharmacists who can answer questions about dosage, side effects, interactions, and safe use of your prescribed medicines. You can contact our pharmacy team via phone or email for professional guidance.
